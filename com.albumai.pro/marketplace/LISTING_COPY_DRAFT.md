## Public name

## Subtitle

Album workflow automation for Photoshop

## Description

AlbumAI Pro helps photographers and album designers manage project photos,
organize ordered PSD templates, place selected photos into Smart Object slots,
and render multi-template album workflows in Adobe Photoshop.

It includes manual sheet design, Smart Auto-Flow, Smart Typography,
transactional PSD and JPEG output, cancellation, recovery, resume, and safe
retry. AlbumAI Pro works offline by default and does not require a third-party
service.
